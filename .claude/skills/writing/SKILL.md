# Skill: /writing-shape
Analyze the target documentation, PRD, or blog post requirements. Define the high-level skeleton, macro structure, and organizational flow. Do not write full paragraphs.

# Skill: /writing-beats
Take the established shape and flesh out the thematic "beats" or talking points for each section. Detail the evidence, data, or technical details needed for each section.

# Skill: /writing-fragments
Refine the raw beats into polished text. Focus purely on tone, eliminating fluff, short declarative pacing, and precise language.
