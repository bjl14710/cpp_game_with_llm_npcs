Goal: A shared world-time value and NPC schedules driven by it, plus a
day/night visual cycle. This should establish the "world bus" pattern that
gossip and the journal will also plug into later, not a one-off clock.

Context: NPCs currently have no purposeful movement tied to time of day.
Build a single authoritative world_time value that everything reads from —
NPC schedule logic, day/night lighting/rendering, anything else time-based
later — rather than each system tracking its own private notion of time.

Scope:
1. World clock — one shared world_time value, advancing at a defined rate,
   exposed as part of the same shared-state store that later gossip/journal
   tickets will use (design the store generically now: a place other
   systems can read/write shared facts, with world_time as its first entry).
2. NPC schedules — each NPC gets a simple schedule (location + activity per
   time-of-day range). Schedule logic reads world_time; it must not track
   its own separate clock.
3. Day/night visuals — lighting/color shift driven by the same world_time
   value, not a separate timer.
