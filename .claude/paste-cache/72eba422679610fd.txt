/idea

Goal: NPCs can learn facts from talking to the player or each other, and
those facts can spread — tell the baker a secret, the blacksmith can
plausibly bring it up later.

Context: This builds directly on the world-bus store from the schedules
ticket (if shipped) or establishes it now if not. Facts should be structured
records — {fact_id, subject, content, source, world_time_learned} — not
free paragraphs, because contradiction-checking in the journal ticket
depends on comparable, normalized fields.

Scope:
1. Fact writing — after a conversation, the LLM proposes new facts as
   structured output (same discipline as affinity_delta from the
   persistence work: propose, then validate, then commit — never write
   directly from LLM output).
2. Fact reading — NPC persona prompts pull relevant facts from the shared
   store before responding, filtered to what that NPC plausibly knows.
3. Propagation rules — decide how a fact spreads from one NPC to another
   (proximity, social ties, elapsed world_time) so gossip isn't instant
   and universal. This is what preserves mystery/investigation pacing if
   that's the direction the story goes.
4. Do not implement this as direct NPC-to-NPC message passing. It must go
   through the shared store so the journal ticket can plug into the same
   mechanism without new wiring.
