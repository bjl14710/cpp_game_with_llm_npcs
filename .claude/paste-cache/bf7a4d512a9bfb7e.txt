/idea

Goal: NPCs can talk to each other, and the player can form a small group —
ask an NPC to follow you, walk to another NPC, and have a three-way (or
N-way) conversation instead of strictly one-on-one.

Context: Builds on the shared world-fact bus and per-NPC memory. Group
conversation is turn-based over the same structured-JSON dialogue pipeline:
each turn, one participant's persona prompt is assembled (their persona +
traits + their view of relevant facts + the conversation so far) and they
respond. NPC-to-NPC exchanges write facts to the world bus like any other
conversation.

Scope:
1. Follow mechanic — ask an NPC (subject to affinity/persona willingness)
   to follow; simple follow movement.
2. Group conversation state — a participant list instead of a single NPC;
   speaking order resolved simply (round-robin or addressed-by-name);
   player messages can address the group or a named participant.
3. NPC-to-NPC turns — when it's an NPC's turn responding to another NPC,
   same pipeline, with the other NPC's last line as input. Cap consecutive
   NPC-to-NPC turns (e.g. 2–3) before returning to the player so
   conversations don't run away without them.
4. Ambient NPC-to-NPC chatter (two NPCs whose schedules put them together
   exchanging a line or two the player can overhear) — include only if it
   falls out naturally from the group mechanism; otherwise list as
   follow-up, don't force it.
5. Memory/fact writes — each participant's private memory updates from
   their own perspective; shared facts revealed in group conversation go to
   the world bus attributed to whoever said them.

Autonomy instructions — unattended run:
- No clarifying questions or /grill-me-style interrogation; pick the most
  defensible option, log reasoning, keep going.
- Watch latency: N participants means N model calls per round. Keep group
  size capped small (3–4) and log observed per-turn latency in the report.
- Follow existing CLAUDE.md agent routing. Draft PR only, no merge, no
  main. Standard gitignore rules apply.
