Vocab: Tiling Modular Kits over a Parametric Grid

modular kit
An asset pack designed around interchangeable, same-footprint pieces (road straight/corner/junction) that butt together seamlessly. The kit's contract: origin-centered, uniform bounds, consistent texel density.
tile pitch
The center-to-center spacing of placed tiles. Here pitch = street width (16), chosen so junction landmarks land on tile centers.
grid anchoring
Choosing the tile grid's origin/phase so that world landmarks (junctions, doorways) coincide with tile centers or edges, rather than starting the grid at an arbitrary map edge.
end cap
A partial tile fitted (via the Fill contract) to the remainder left when a span isn't an integer number of tiles — 6 units here, at the ±110 edge.
parametric layout
A world defined by named constants (block pitch 64, street width 16, half-size 110) so derived systems — tiling, colliders, crossings — compute placements instead of hardcoding coordinates.
z-fighting
Flicker when two coplanar surfaces contest the depth buffer. Avoided here by floating tiles 0.01 above the grass plane and drawing shared junction tiles exactly once.
